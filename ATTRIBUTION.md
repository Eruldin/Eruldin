# Düşüş: Choralim Protocol — Asset Credits

## Pixel Art Tileset

**Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup — Full Tileset (CC0 / Public Domain)**

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-crawl-32x32-tiles
Mirror: https://github.com/crawl/tiles/tree/master/releases

Originally based on rltiles (http://rltiles.sourceforge.net/), extended by the
Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup team (http://crawl.develz.org/wordpress/).

Released under CC0 — no attribution required; credited here with gratitude.

Artists who gave approval for their work to be released under CC0:

- Eino Keskitalo (evktalo)
- David Lawrence Ramsey (dolorous)
- Enne Walker (ennewalker)
- Poor_Yurik
- Stefan O'Rear (stefanor)
- and the wider DCSS tiles contributors (see LICENSE.txt in
  `art_src/dcss/Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup Full/`)

Used for: character/enemy/boss sprites, floor & wall tiles, doors, props,
decals, item/boon icons, portraits, and particle sprites, processed by
`art_src/build_art.py` into `art/`.

## Everything else

- Music & SFX: procedurally synthesized at runtime (`src/audio.gd`)
- Fonts, UI chrome, vignette/CRT overlays: generated in-engine (`src/pixel.gd`, `src/ui.gd`)
